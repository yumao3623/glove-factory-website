# Site Architecture V1 (Phase 1.1 Historical Recommendation)

> This file records the reviewed Phase 1.1 provisional recommendation. The Phase 2 freeze is authoritative in `PROJECT_SPEC_V1.md` and `ROUTE_REGISTRY.md`.

## Status and evidence

This is a Phase 1.1 research recommendation, not an Architecture Freeze or build brief. Every URL is a `PROVISIONAL_ROUTE`.

`PROVISIONAL_RESEARCH_SCOPE`: United States / English is primary; United Kingdom / English is secondary validation. This is not a final commercial-market decision.

Product scope and general customization availability are `FACTORY_CONFIRMED`. 1688 titles and cumulative sales are `FACTORY_PLATFORM_EVIDENCE` for domestic-platform assortment and relative traction only. Keyword metrics are `THIRD_PARTY_SEMRUSH_INTERFACE`; Google live SERP is separate `PLATFORM_EVIDENCE`. Architecture recommendations are `INFERENCE`.

## Homepage positioning options

The homepage must cover bridal, evening/formal, costume, kids dress gloves and veils without binding `/` to one product keyword. Phase 2 must select and validate the final language; Phase 1.1 does not freeze an H1.

- Fashion & Occasion Gloves Manufacturer
- Bridal, Evening & Costume Gloves Manufacturer
- Gloves & Bridal Accessories Manufacturer
- Occasion Gloves & Wedding Veils Manufacturer

Each option needs legal-identity and public-claim review. None is approved copy.

## Style and material ownership

| Term | V1 role | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Satin / Satin Opera | Child collection or substantial module under `/opera-gloves/` | No top-level Satin family. A child route is conditional on meaningful assortment. |
| Lace | Cross-family material/style facet; qualified Bridal child or module | No generic `/lace-gloves/`; the query is polluted despite high volume. |
| Sheer / Tulle | Qualified style/material child or module under relevant family | No generic `/mesh-gloves/`. |
| Fingerless | Style child/filter, primarily under Bridal and relevant Costume products | No generic `/fingerless-gloves/`; non-target intent dominates. |

## Pages not recommended

- No separate `/wedding-gloves/`; it would compete with `/bridal-gloves/`.
- No separate `/opera-gloves-wholesale/`, `/opera-gloves-manufacturer/` or supplier variant.
- No separate Bridal wholesale/manufacturer/supplier variants.
- No generic Lace, Mesh, Satin or Fingerless top-level page.
- No broad Blog/Guides hub until a factual editorial program and owner exist.

## Architecture principle

`High-fit Product Keyword -> Product / Collection Landing Page -> B2B Manufacturing Evidence -> RFQ Conversion`

Low-volume procurement modifiers support on-page B2B evidence and conversion. They do not create automatic page ownership.
