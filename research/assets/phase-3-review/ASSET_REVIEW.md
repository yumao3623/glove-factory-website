## Handling rules

- No supplied ZIP is copied into `public/`.
- Marketplace product images remain review evidence until the factory confirms reuse permission.
- Product descriptions must state only visible style/material cues; photography does not verify composition, certification, capacity or performance.
- Do not use Disney, Frozen or Elsa in product names, alt text, SEO, or page copy.
- The detailed file-level inventory, SHA-256 values and contact sheets are generated by `scripts/audit_asset_images.py`.
