# Competitor UX/UI Review

Source set: Phase 1 public observations on 2026-08-31. These are UX patterns, not claims about the factory. Reviewed groups: Wedding Factory Direct, Mariell Online, Wona Trading, CB Flowers Crafts, Cornelia James and Grace Loves Lace.

| area | observed pattern | implication for this site |
|---|---|---|
| Navigation | Wholesale sites use department > category > style/length; retail specialists use occasion/material navigation. | Use Products hub > five families, then controlled style/material modules. Keep RFQ visible. |
| Hero | Retail sites lead with product photography and occasion language; wholesale sites lead with category breadth or buyer gate. | Lead with real product range plus a direct B2B enquiry action; avoid unsupported metrics or price claims. |
| Collection grid | Repeated product cards, material/length labels, sorting or filters. | Use stable 4:5/1:1 cards, confirmed labels and no fake stock/price. |
| Filters | Material, length, color and occasion are common; wholesale sites also sort by SKU/availability. | Filters are progressive enhancement and non-indexable by default. Only expose fields with enough data. |
| Trust | Address, business hours, consultation/appointment, sourcing or fulfillment statements appear. | Show only approved factory evidence with source/date; do not imitate competitor heritage, sustainability or shipping claims. |
| Factory evidence | Reviewed wholesale/retail competitors did not provide comparable verified factory proof. | A factual process/evidence block is a differentiator once the factory supplies publishable proof. |
| Customization | Cornelia James shows swatches/fit consultation; Grace Loves Lace explains styling and FAQ; B2B competitors rarely show OEM workflow. | Define an explicit custom-manufacturing path and RFQ context, bounded by confirmed capabilities. |
| RFQ/contact | Wholesale sites expose email/phone or account gates; retail sites use appointments/chat. | One `/contact/` RFQ flow with email required; WhatsApp optional and pending. |
| Mobile | Catalogs collapse navigation and grids; appointment CTAs remain prominent. | Mobile disclosure nav, 2-column cards where readable, 44px targets and sticky-but-nonblocking RFQ action. |
| Footer | Category links, contact, policy and business details are common. | Include five families, Custom, Factory, Contact and Privacy; omit unknown address/phone. |
| Typography/space | Premium retail uses expressive type and generous whitespace; wholesale uses denser utility layouts. | Adopt Editorial Utility: serif display only, sans body, scan-friendly density. |
| Photography | Retail uses styled editorial images; catalogs use isolated product shots. | Prioritize real product originals, with consistent crops plus detail/process evidence. |

## Guardrails

Do not copy brand voice, layouts, product data, prices, trade policies, claims, photography or visual identity. Marketplace and retailer patterns are reference evidence only. The project remains a manufacturer-facing B2B site, not a retail storefront.
