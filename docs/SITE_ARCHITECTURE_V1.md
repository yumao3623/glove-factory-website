## Excluded or deferred from V1

| Candidate | Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Generic `Lace Gloves` page | Do not create | Search results are mixed fashion/DIY/non-bridal. Bridal lace is the correct qualified owner. |
| Generic `Mesh Gloves` page | Do not create | SERP is industrial, sport and hunting mixed. |
| Generic `Fingerless Gloves` page | Do not create | Demand and SERP are dominated by winter/work/tactical/non-target categories. |
| Standalone first-level Fingerless category | Do not create | Qualified bridal term is valid but better supported as a Bridal child/subcategory. |
| Veils / Bridal Accessories core hub | Defer | High demand does not offset only-medium current product-fit evidence. Confirm active manufacturing range first. |
| Broad Guides / Blog hub | Defer | No ongoing factual editorial program exists. Record future guide tasks only after source ownership is available. |
| Retail checkout, price pages, account pages | Out of scope | Project is B2B lead generation; no approved commercial/pricing model. |

## Freeze blockers

The research phase is sufficient to propose the page ownership above. The architecture must not be frozen until the factory confirms active product families, whether veils are a strategic active range, publishable customization/OEM boundaries, legal English identity and enquiry ownership. MOQ, capacity and lead time are not architecture blockers unless the team intends to publicly promise them.
