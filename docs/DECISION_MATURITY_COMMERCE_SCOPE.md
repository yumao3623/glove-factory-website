# Decision: mature commerce scope

**Date:** 2026-09-12

The owner has explicitly requested that the former preview-only, RFQ-first boundary evolve toward a mature international B2B commerce site model inspired by Cornelia James. This decision authorizes planning and local implementation of catalogue filtering, PDP, cart, account and payment integration seams.

This supersedes the *non-goal* wording in the earlier preview documents for future work, while preserving their historical evidence and approved-product boundaries. It does not authorize Supabase, Stripe, email, DNS, analytics, deployment or other external mutations. Those remain gated by credentials, ownership, privacy/legal review and Human acceptance.

Implementation must preserve source provenance, keep unconfirmed factory/commercial facts out of public copy, and label local checkout/payment as preview until sandbox evidence exists.
