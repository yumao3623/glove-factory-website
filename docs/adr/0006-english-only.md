# ADR 0006: English Only In V1

## Context
US English is the primary research scope, with UK validation; translation and enquiry ownership for other languages are not established.

## Decision
Publish English only. Keep locale boundaries extensible but emit no additional language pages or hreflang.

## Alternatives
Chinese plus English, or a four-language launch.

## Consequences
Lower content and QA burden, but reduced non-English reach until operations exist.

## Change trigger
Approved market owner, translated source content, response capability and equivalent page maintenance for a second locale.
