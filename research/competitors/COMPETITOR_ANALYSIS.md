# Competitor Analysis

## Scope and classification

These are Google-SERP-discovered competitors, not factory facts. Public pages were observed read-only on 2026-08-31. `MANUFACTURER_COMPETITOR` is deliberately empty: none of the reviewed pages supplied enough public evidence to classify it as a comparable manufacturer for this project. A competitor's public production claim is not evidence about the factory.

| Group | Domains | Why they matter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wholesale competitors | weddingfactorydirect.com; mariellonline.com; wonatrading.com; cbflowerscrafts.com | Recur in US wholesale-result sets and demonstrate wholesale category/page forms. |
| SEO competitors | corneliajames.com; graceloveslace.com | Recur across commercial bridal, opera, satin, lace and fingerless queries with focused category/product pages. |
| Marketplaces, not direct competitors | amazon.com; etsy.com; faire.com; DHgate | Occupy result space but are not comparable owned B2B sites. |

## Non-brand visibility observation

The evidence supports **page-level** rather than domain-traffic conclusions. The repeated non-brand entry points are wholesale glove categories for wholesale sites and bridal/occasion/material collections for direct retailers. No current third-party domain-organic module was readable; estimated domain traffic, keyword positions, backlinks and top organic page totals are therefore recorded as `Not available` or `Not observed`, never invented.

## What the observed pages do well

- Wholesale pages use clear category labels, style/length subcategories, SKU/product grids and direct commercial actions.
- Cornelia James uses the English product vocabulary that the SERP rewards: bridal, satin, tulle, lace, opera, material and length.
- Grace Loves Lace demonstrates information gain on a collection page with guidance, FAQs and a clear next action, rather than relying on a thin grid.

## Gaps and guarded opportunities

- Catalog/retail pages leave an opening for a supplier-facing path that explains product scope, enquiry process and customization boundaries with approved proof.
- The project must not imitate competitor claims about handmade production, sustainability, heritage, delivery or pricing. Those claims remain competitor-specific.
- A B2B site should not use `custom bridal gloves` as its primary SEO justification: the US SERP is consumer personalization-led. Its Custom Manufacturing page should own a buyer-service task, using qualified B2B language only after the scope is confirmed.
