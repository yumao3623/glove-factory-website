# Phase 3 Checkpoint 1 Refinement

Status: `HUMAN_REVIEW_ACCEPTED_FOR_CHECKPOINT_1`

This record evaluates the post-gate Human Review without changing the frozen Phase 2 architecture, route ownership, product families, SEO scope, catalogue model, or publishing boundaries.

> **Historical record - superseded by Human Review on 2026-09-04.** The original `Header/Footer only` limitation for `JS Meilai` remains here as a record of the earlier decision, but is no longer current. Human approval now permits the public English brand and metadata title. The original checkpoint is not otherwise rewritten.

## Decisions

| Review area | Decision | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Variant E direction | `ACCEPT` | The approved image-led editorial system has distinct desktop and mobile compositions, carries the range through real visual-gate media, and keeps the RFQ path clear. Refinement is more appropriate than another visual-direction cycle. |
| Prototype-like copy | `ACCEPT` | Section copy that explained the composition was replaced with direct buyer-facing sourcing language. Development and permission boundaries remain explicit only where they are required to avoid publishing unconfirmed content. |
| Products Hub hero imagery | `RETAIN_CURRENT` | The products hub is a fast range-navigation surface. Its text-led hero provides a deliberate calm transition before the image-led family grid, so adding an ornamental hero image would not improve its primary job. |
| Bridal veil contextual image | `PARTIALLY_ACCEPT` | The veil remains a single supporting image in the material-context collage, never a card or collection hero. This supports the confirmed wedding-accessory ecosystem without displacing the Bridal Gloves H1, hero, grid, or canonical intent. |
| Factory evidence location | `PARTIALLY_ACCEPT` | `/factory/` is the frozen owner for comprehensive factory/about evidence. Until that route is implemented, the homepage retains a shorter, clearly bounded workshop-evidence bridge to establish manufacturer identity; collection pages retain only one restrained bridge rather than a full factory story. |
| Product versus factory responsibilities | `ACCEPT` | Collection and hub pages answer what a buyer can source. The future `/factory/` page will be the primary owner for approved process and trust evidence, consistent with the Route Registry and Project Spec. |
| Spacing, crop and visual balance | `ACCEPT` | Secondary section padding was reduced on mobile and the factory band was tightened. The image mosaic, asymmetric family layout, product-card ratios, and black hero treatment were retained because they are part of the approved E visual language. |
| Mobile density | `PARTIALLY_ACCEPT` | Supporting bands now use tighter mobile padding. The hero and family imagery retain breathing room because compressing them would undo the approved image-led composition. |
| Repeated black bands | `DEFER` | The current three routes remain balanced. Variation across the remaining collection templates should be driven by the family imagery and search intent after those routes enter a later checkpoint, rather than adding arbitrary color changes now. |
| Development product references | `RETAIN_CURRENT` | Fixture names and descriptions remain marked as development references. They are not treated as approved public product names or specifications. |
| Future collection templates | `ACCEPT` | Shared primitives are typography, image treatment, navigation, CTA language, RFQ handling and spacing. Future collection layout and media rhythm must be adapted to their own intent and approved material; the Bridal composition is not a mandatory copy-and-relabel template. |
| Hero image separation | `PARTIALLY_ACCEPT` | The square-edge collage remains an intentional editorial motif, but its black gutters were reduced from `8px` to `4px` in the Homepage and Bridal E heroes. This keeps image hierarchy and honest cropping while avoiding a heavy tile effect, particularly on mobile. |
| Domain status at the checkpoint | `ACCEPT` | At the time of this checkpoint, `jsmeilai.com` was only a planning candidate. It is now confirmed as the official domain, while runtime canonical URLs correctly continue to use the local development origin or an explicitly supplied environment variable until the publication-integration checkpoint. |
| Provisional brand display | `ACCEPT` | `JS Meilai` replaces the visual placeholder in the Header and Footer only. It is a development-stage display treatment derived from the current factory/domain context, not an approved English legal identity, canonical host, metadata value or public-contact fact. |

## Implemented Refinements

- Replaced design-explanation copy with concise buyer-facing headings and sourcing language on the homepage, Products hub, family discovery module and Bridal collection.
- Kept development, permission and unconfirmed-fact status language where it protects the publishing boundary.
- Reduced vertical padding in supporting homepage and Bridal sections at mobile widths while retaining desktop editorial scale.
- Reframed workshop sections as `Manufacturing evidence` and limited them to a small visual bridge instead of a broad factory narrative.
- Retained the Bridal collection's glove-first hero and product grid; the sole veil image remains contextual.
- Refined only the Homepage and Bridal E hero mosaics with narrower black image gutters; no rounded card treatment, blur, or site-wide media effect was introduced.
- Recorded `jsmeilai.com` as a domain-planning candidate without creating a production resource or changing canonical output. Its official-domain status was confirmed after this checkpoint; see `INFRASTRUCTURE_REGISTRY.md` for the current state.
- Replaced the Header and Footer placeholder with the visual-only provisional display `JS Meilai`, held in one local constant for consistency.

## Deliberate Non-Changes

- No new routes or factory page were created.
- No claim, metric, MOQ, sample term, lead time, capacity, certification, approved English legal identity or contact detail was invented.
- No asset provenance or permission status changed.
- No ecommerce behavior, database, CMS, payment, price, cart, stock or rating was introduced.
