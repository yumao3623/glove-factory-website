## Opportunity lenses

1. **Traffic:** Wedding Veils, Bridal Veils, Opera Gloves, Lace Gloves and Wedding Gloves have visible demand, but fit and intent control ownership.
2. **Commercial:** Five confirmed product families have commercial SERPs and a credible product-collection page form.
3. **B2B Money:** Exact wholesale terms are retained as buyer language and conversion signals, not as a separate page-generation formula.
