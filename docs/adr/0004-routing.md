# ADR 0004: Product-Family Canonical Routing

## Context
Phase 1.1 found five distinct commercial families but polluted generic material and modifier SERPs.

## Decision
Freeze five family routes plus one products hub, one custom-manufacturing route, one factory/about route and one contact route. No duplicate wholesale/manufacturer/supplier URLs.

## Alternatives
Material-first taxonomy, modifier pages, or a single generic gloves page.

## Consequences
Clear ownership and internal linking; style children require a measurable threshold and maintenance commitment.

## Change trigger
Sustained distinct SERP intent and approved assortment justify a new canonical child, recorded by ADR.
