# Keyword Research Seed (not final keyword map)

Source: `Keyword Stats 2026-08-26 at 17_46_17.xlsx`, Google export covering 2025-08-01 to 2026-07-31.

## Dataset signal

- 1,989 usable keyword rows were read.
- 1,731 rows have average monthly searches of 50; 187 have 500; 26 have 5,000; two have 50,000; 43 are zero/blank.
- 1,456 rows contain `fingerless`, `half finger`, `open finger` or `without fingers` language.
- 470 rows contain bridal/formal signals such as `bridal`, `wedding`, `lace`, `opera`, `tulle`, `satin`, `sheer`, `mesh`, `evening`, `party` or `veil`.
- The export uses Chinese competition labels and includes brand/retail/local modifiers, so it cannot be used as a final B2B keyword map.

## Candidate seed clusters

### Product-fit core

`bridal gloves`, `wedding gloves`, `lace gloves`, `lace bridal gloves`, `white bridal gloves`, `satin gloves`, `wedding satin gloves`, `opera gloves`, `lace opera gloves`, `long lace gloves`, `mesh gloves`, `sheer wedding gloves`, `tulle bridal gloves`, `evening gloves`, `formal gloves`

### B2B modifiers

`wholesale bridal gloves`, `bridal gloves wholesale`, `wholesale lace gloves`, `custom bridal gloves`, `custom wedding gloves`, `custom fingerless gloves`, `fingerless gloves wholesale`, `fingerless gloves bulk`, `wedding gloves manufacturer`, `bridal gloves supplier`, `glove factory`, `private label gloves`, `OEM gloves`

### Secondary / intent split required

`fingerless gloves`, `fingerless gloves women`, `cashmere fingerless gloves`, `fingerless mittens`, `black fingerless gloves`, `fingerless leather gloves`, `thermal fingerless gloves`, `fingerless work gloves`, `fingerless motorcycle gloves`

The broad fingerless head term has 50,000 average monthly searches in this export, but the product and SERP intent may be mostly consumer/winter/work/sports. It must not become the primary site keyword without SERP validation.

### Exclude or quarantine during first pass

Brand terms (`gucci`, `chanel`, `zara`, `ugg`, `carhartt`, `vera wang`), local terms (`near me`), marketplace terms (`amazon`, `etsy`, `shein`) and unrelated utility/safety terms should not define the factory site architecture.

## Scoring rule for next phase

`Product Fit > Search Intent > Commercial Value > SERP Fit > Volume / KD / CPC`

Low-volume manufacturer, supplier, wholesale and custom phrases remain eligible when their buying intent and SERP page type fit the factory.
